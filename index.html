From first
conversation
to closure.

Nine steps across three phases. The third phase, partnership, is where we stay with you through implementation, support and evaluation.

Pre-engagement

  1. Initial exploration

    Identify objectives, stakeholders and potential areas of collaboration.

  2. Preliminary assessment

    Evaluate feasibility, challenges and opportunities.

  3. Proposal & alignment

    A formal proposal of scope and deliverables, agreed by all parties.

Engagement

  1. Client discovery

    Belief audits, focus interviews and data analysis to find the patterns that matter.

  2. Solution design

    Design and develop the solution with your team.

  3. Presentation & discussion

    Present, gather feedback and refine together.

Partnership

  1. Implementation strategy

    Plan the steps, resources and timelines for implementation.

  2. Execution & support

    Put the plan into action, monitor progress and support the rollout.

  3. Evaluation & closure

    Measure against objectives, close formally and document the lessons.
