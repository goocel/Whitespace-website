TokenValueMeaning
--gutterclamp(16px, 4vw, 72px)Equal left and right space: 72px on desktop, 4% of the screen below that, 16px minimum on phones.
--container1760pxMaximum content width. Header, hero, sections and footer all share it, so their edges line up.
--section-spaceclamp(72px, 8vw, 120px)Top and bottom padding of every section (max 240px between sections).
--head-spaceclamp(48px, 6vw, 80px)Gap between a section heading and its content.
--header-h64pxSticky header height.
--tile-gap3pxGap between gray tiles (cards, cells, steps).
