Case studies

AI at work.

Real work, client names withheld. In every case AI does the reading and the matching; people make the final call.

IT diagnostic · Real work

One evidence-based view of IT.

Objective: assess IT maturity across several companies, consistently and backed by evidence. The documents and interview transcripts were far more than a team could read by hand.

  1. The evidence

    Documents and interview transcripts, for every company.

  2. AI interprets

    Reads every page, tags it to seven dimensions, drafts findings with sources.

  3. Code calculates

    The same arithmetic, every time, on every company.

  4. People review

    Assessors check the system; people make the final judgment.

Invoice OCR · Illustrative flow

From a mountain of paper to checked records.

A finance team was spending most of its time on paperwork and data entry: invoices, delivery notes and receipts stacked up waiting to be keyed, scanned and checked by hand.

People move from keying data to judgment: exceptions, suppliers, process improvement.

  1. Paper arrives

    Invoice, delivery note, receipt.

  2. AI reads & matches

    Supplier, reference and amount, checked against each other.

  3. Journal

    Checked records, ready for finance.

  4. A person decides

    Every exception goes to a person.

Tender engine · Illustrative flow

Read the RFP once, not again and again.

Every team used to re-read the same long tender document for its part of the bid. Now one engine reads it once into a structured record that every team works from.

  1. The tender

    One long document.

  2. Tender engine

    Reads it once and extracts the requirements.

  3. Every team, one record

    Project, finance, legal, operations and procurement.

  4. Proposal

    Built by the bid team. People still decide the bid.
