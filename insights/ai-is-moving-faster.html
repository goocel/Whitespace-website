AI is moving faster than any team can track

New uses arrive every month. Using AI yourself is easy. Putting it into business processes is not.

Type
Perspective

New uses every month

Not long ago, AI meant a chat window. Today the same tools draft and reply to email, read long PDFs, build slides, write spreadsheet formulas, create and edit images, write and fix code, research and summarise, turn meetings into notes and actions, analyse and chart data, and even build a whole product.

Agents: the newest

Agents don't just answer, they take actions. That moves the question from "what can AI tell us?" to "which steps of our work can AI carry out, and who checks them?"

The hard part is the process, not the tool

Using AI yourself is easy. Putting it into business processes is not. That takes process know-how and real flight hours, not more tools: knowing where the work really flows, where the exceptions sit and who has to sign off.

The goal is not "using AI". It is hours saved, faster decisions and fewer manual steps.
