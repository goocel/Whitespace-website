Case study · AI Implementation

IT diagnostic: one evidence-based view of IT

Assessing IT maturity across several companies: consistently, and backed by evidence.

Type
Case study
Service
AI Implementation
Note
Real work · client name withheld
Published
October 2026

The challenge

The objective was to assess IT maturity across several companies, in the same way for each one, with every finding traceable to evidence. The evidence was documents and interview transcripts: far more than a team can read by hand.

How it works

  1. The evidence

    Documents and interview transcripts, for every company.

  2. AI interprets

    Reads every page, tags it to seven dimensions, drafts findings with sources.

  3. Code calculates

    The same arithmetic, every time, on every company.

  4. People review

    Assessors check the system; people make the final judgment.

Seven dimensions

  • 1 · Business–IT alignment
  • 2 · Service operations
  • 3 · Development & delivery
  • 4 · Architecture
  • 5 · Resilience
  • 6 · Organisation
  • 7 · Emerging technology
AI does the reading, code does the arithmetic, and people make the final judgment.
