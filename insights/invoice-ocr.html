Case study · AI Implementation

Invoice OCR: from a mountain of paper to checked records

A finance team was spending most of its time on paperwork and data entry.

Type
Case study
Service
AI Implementation
Note
Illustrative flow · client name withheld
Published
October 2026

The challenge

Invoices, delivery notes and receipts arrived on paper and stacked up waiting to be keyed. Every document went through the same manual line: scan, key, check, by hand.

How it works

  1. Paper arrives

    Invoice, delivery note, receipt.

  2. AI reads & matches

    Supplier, reference and amount, checked against each other.

  3. Journal

    Checked records, ready for finance.

  4. A person decides

    Every exception goes to a person.

What changes for the team

The routine matching runs on its own. What is left for people is the work that needs judgment: the exceptions, the conversations with suppliers and improving the process itself.

People move from keying data to judgment: exceptions, suppliers, process improvement.
