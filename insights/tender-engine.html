Tender engine: read the RFP once, not again and again

Every team used to re-read the same long tender document for its part of the bid.

Type
Case study
Service
AI Implementation
Note

The challenge

A tender is one long document, and project, finance, legal, operations and procurement each need a different part of it. Before, every team re-read the whole thing to find its part of the bid.

How it works

  1. The tender

    One long document.

  2. Tender engine

    Reads it once and extracts the requirements.

  3. Every team, one record

    Project, finance, legal, operations and procurement.

  4. Proposal

    Built by the bid team. People still decide the bid.

What the engine does

  • Tender checklist
  • Solution doc auto-fill
  • Client BoQ conversion
  • Client research & scoring
